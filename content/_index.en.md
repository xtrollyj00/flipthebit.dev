# About the Flip the Bit

I’m an FPGA engineer with a passion for low-level programming :gear: and a slight aversion to web technologies :globe_with_meridians::upside_down_face:.
That’s exactly why I decided to start this blog — a place for anything that involves flipping bits with intent.

From IP core design and functional verification :microscope: to microcontrollers :hammer_and_wrench: and the use of AI :robot: in hardware engineering,
I write about the things I build, learn, and experiment with.

This blog is written in my free time :coffee:, so posts may appear on a somewhat irregular schedule. Still, I hope to share
useful knowledge, practical insights, and real-world experiences from development along the way.

**[So come and read the posts...](./blog) :rocket:**
